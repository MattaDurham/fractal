 FRACTAL

Follow the words.

Choose folder opens a folder, drive or vault on this computer. Saved folders stay in your local collection list. Rescan when files change.

Scroll or pinch toward a word. Smaller words appear inside it; keep going until you reach folders and files. Drag to pan. Click for details; double-click to step inside or inspect a document.

Folders follows your tree. By type groups files by extension. Document tables reveal cached source previews; PDFs can spread into pages.

Your files stay on this computer. No account, cloud upload or remote rendering service.

Keyboard: + / − zoom, arrows pan, 0 fits, Enter enters a selected folder, Escape goes up, Home returns to the collection. Browse this level provides equivalent buttons.
